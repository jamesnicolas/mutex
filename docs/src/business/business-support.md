# Business Support

For billing and business support (account setup, invoices, organization questions), email [billing-support@mutex.dev](mailto:billing-support@mutex.dev). Business support is prioritized relative to other support channels.

For general questions, email [hi@mutex.dev](mailto:hi@mutex.dev).

## Open-source issues

Questions and bugs about the Mutex editor itself (features, extensions, language support, crashes) go through the main Mutex project on [GitHub](https://github.com/zed-industries/zed/issues).
